Implementation refinement following independent review
- Short affirmative interpretation accepts only fully parsed bounded clauses:
  exact affirmation, current-material referential inspection, complete no-effect
  constraints, and continue/return-result instruction. Unrecognized tails remain
  unresolved. It is not a general natural-language inference engine.
- Explicit scope selection also validates the complete first clause and tails;
  an unknown different environment cannot hide after a recognized scope.
- Unknown scope propositions and explicit withdrawal invalidate the old selection
  even when the current question is unrelated; ordinary unrelated answers retain it.
- The existing saved question/digest/answer is sufficient interpretation evidence;
  no new persisted observation shape, journal key or frozen contract is changed.
- Initial scope-message extraction remains unchanged and is a separate limitation.
- Both live confirmation forms are now explicit regression fixtures. '是否按'
  is a positive yes/no proposition, not a denial. A second policy name in its
  tail is recognized only as an exact title/first heading from the already-loaded
  POLICY with that scope. Unknown/other-scope policy labels remain unresolved.
